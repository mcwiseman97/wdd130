The Salmon River Run

Experience the "River of No Return." This trip features Class III rapids perfect for families and intermediate paddlers. Enjoy breathtaking Idaho scenery and nightly beach camping.

The Grand Canyon Expedition

The ultimate bucket list trip. Navigate legendary rapids like Crystal and Lava Falls. This 7-day journey offers a deep dive into the geology and history of the Colorado River.

Snake River Sprint

Short on time? This high-intensity day trip through the Snake River Canyon offers nonstop action and stunning basalt cliffs. Perfect for adrenaline junkies.

Alabama Made-Up River

Wanna go to a place that does not exist. Put on some VR glasses and see where AI takes you. Room comes with moving chairs, water and air blasting to mimic the sensation of actually riding on a river.
